The Artist

Bubba Irwin

With more than two decades behind the machine, Bubba Irwin has built a reputation as one of the most versatile and dedicated tattoo artists working today. From large-scale black and grey realism to full-color surrealism, his portfolio reflects a career spent refining every discipline of the craft — never settling into a single lane.

Bubba's work is grounded in a simple philosophy: every tattoo should be built to last a lifetime. That means clean structure, deliberate composition, and honest collaboration with every client. A tattoo isn't just an image — it's a permanent piece of someone's story, and it deserves an artist who treats it that way.

Alongside his client work, Bubba is a studio owner and mentor, helping shape the next generation of artists through his affiliated shops — Old Town Ink and Glasshouse Tattoo — where the standard is the same as his own: uncompromising quality, a clean professional environment, and art that holds up decades from now.

Consultations & Booking

Let's Build Your Tattoo

Tell Bubba what you have in mind. Share your idea, where you want it, and any reference images — the more detail, the better. Every inquiry goes straight to Bubba and his team, and you'll hear back about consultation availability, sizing, and pricing.

  • All work is custom — references are a starting point, not a template.
  • Large-scale pieces (sleeves, back pieces) are booked as multi-session projects.
  • Deposits are required to secure appointment dates.

Prefer email? Reach out directly at Bubba@bubbairwintattoo.com
