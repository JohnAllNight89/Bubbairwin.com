The Artist

Bubba Irwin

Aaron "Bubba" Irwin is an elite American tattoo artist known worldwide for his technical precision, structural depth, and mastery of realistic artwork and detailed portraits. Entirely self-taught through intense trial, error, and dedicated practice at home, Bubba forged a distinct artistic path that completely bypassed traditional gatekeepers — launching him to the absolute top of the industry and leading him to establish world-class destinations like Old Town Ink in Scottsdale, Arizona, and Glasshouse Tattoo, located inside Jelly Roll's Goodnight Nashville bar on Broadway.

Bubba's work is grounded in a simple philosophy: every tattoo should be built to last a lifetime. Clean structure, deliberate composition, and honest collaboration with every client — because a tattoo isn't just an image, it's a permanent piece of someone's story.

Meet Bubba: The Human Behind the Ink

When the cameras are off and the shop lights go down, Bubba is just a real, down-to-earth guy who is fiercely devoted to his kids. That deep love for his family shapes everything he does. He brings an unmatched, grind-it-out work ethic to his life and business, fueled by a genuine passion for what he creates and a rare ability to look way past the ink on skin to see the actual person underneath. When you sit in his chair, you aren't just a client — he treats every single person who walks through his doors like a part of his own family. At the end of the day, Bubba is just as proud of being a great dad as he is of being a master of his craft.

The Difference

Why Sit in Bubba's Chair?

When you commission a piece from Aaron "Bubba" Irwin, you aren't just getting a tattoo — you are investing in world-class, museum-grade art engineered to last a lifetime. Here is why elite clients, music icons, and collectors from across the country trust Bubba with their skin.

01

Uncompromising Realism & Technical Mastery

As an Ink Master Season 9 Champion and Season 12 veteran coach, Bubba operates at the absolute apex of the industry. His hyper-realistic portraits and intricate visual depth are the product of decades of self-taught grit, relentless perfectionism, and an unmatched eye for anatomy and contrast. If you want portraiture that actually breathes, commands attention, and defies aging — this is the standard.

02

Trusted by Icons and Superstars

When celebrities and country music royalty — including Jason Aldean, Kane Brown, and Tyler Hubbard — need elite-tier work, they fly in or book out private sessions with Bubba. When you sit in his chair, you are getting the exact same caliber of artistry trusted by people who can afford to work with literally anyone on earth.

03

A Legacy Built on Grit, Integrity, and Devotion

Bubba's entire journey is completely self-made. Without traditional gatekeepers, he forged his technique through intense dedication and a blue-collar work ethic instilled by his father. That same unwavering discipline carries over into how he treats his clients — alongside being a fiercely devoted father who brings that grounded pride and protection to everything he builds.

04

Iconic Destinations, Including Jelly Roll's Broadway Hotspot

Whether you're visiting his co-owned flagship shop Old Town Ink in Scottsdale, Arizona, stepping into Glasshouse — located right inside Jelly Roll's Goodnight Nashville bar on Broadway and co-operated alongside fellow legend Chris Nunez — or booking an ultra-exclusive private luxury experience through Branded Tattoo Co., the environment matches the art: high-end, high-energy, and completely unforgettable.

Let's Build Your Tattoo

Tell Bubba what you have in mind. Share your idea, where you want it, and any reference images — the more detail, the better. Every inquiry goes straight to Bubba and his team, and you'll hear back about consultation availability, sizing, and pricing.

  • All work is custom — references are a starting point, not a template.
  • Large-scale pieces (sleeves, back pieces) are booked as multi-session projects.
  • Deposits are required to secure appointment dates.
