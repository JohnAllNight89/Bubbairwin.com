Get Tattooed on Broadway — Inside Jelly Roll's Goodnight Nashville

Searching for a Nashville tattoo artist who's actually earned the hype? Ink Master winner Aaron "Bubba" Irwin co-owns Glasshouse Tattoo, located inside Jelly Roll's Goodnight Nashville bar on Broadway — co-operated with fellow Ink Master legend Chris Nunez.

World-Class Tattoos in the Heart of Music City

Glasshouse brings championship-level tattooing to the most famous street in country music. It's high-end, high-energy, and completely unforgettable — a working studio where the art meets Broadway's atmosphere, steps from the stage lights inside Goodnight Nashville.

It's no accident that country music royalty — Kane Brown, Brett Young, Jason Aldean, Tyler Hubbard, Gary Allan, Tracy Lawrence — trust Bubba with their skin. In Nashville, the artist the stars book is the artist you can book.

What to Book in Nashville

  • Black & grey realism — from single-session statement pieces to full sleeves
